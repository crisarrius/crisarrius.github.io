Death Poem 2

The hiring manager, the landlord, the woman.
All masks of the same entity
Who takes applications, sits, and decides…
Unapologetically indifferent, with reason unfair,
Rejected are many; the chosen are shared.
Oh, to be loved and breathe that rarefied air.
Unfortunately they’ve decided to go with
someone else on this occasion.
Death will be happy to have me.
